Track_Partitioning_Problem

Input:
    Given 2 tracks of 2 different lengths x and y.

Output: 
    Given a distance of certain length, find total number of different ways to combine the tracks to reach that length and list them out.

E.g. 
    Track length:
    10 13
    Distance:
    + 749
    = 10 * 32 + 13 * 33
    = 10 * 19 + 13 * 43
    = 10 * 6 + 13 * 53
    = 10 * 71 + 13 * 3
    = 10 * 58 + 13 * 13
    = 10 * 45 + 13 * 23
    Total variants: 6
